ULTRAWIDE BAR / GDEYOUNG

The stock bar,
sized for ultrawide.

A fork of omarchy.bar for very wide screens: a partial-width bar centered on the screen, per-monitor width and height, fullscreen auto-hide with edge-peek, transparent mode, and a right-click settings popup. At 100% it is pixel-identical to stock.
  • Partial-width bar, centered on screen
  • Per-monitor width & height overrides
  • Fullscreen auto-hide with edge-peek
  • Transparent bar mode
  • Right-click settings popup
  • CLI: ultrawide-bar-width 60
  • Live reload via omarchy bar set
  • omarchy bar reset restores stock
